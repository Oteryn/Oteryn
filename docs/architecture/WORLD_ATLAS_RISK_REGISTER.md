## Risk acceptance rules

- Any unresolved **Critical** risk with a triggered leading indicator blocks the affected cutover until the owning lifecycle produces mitigating evidence or an explicit later owner decision changes the architecture.
- High risks may not be hidden in a generic `PASS`; they require an exact mitigation result in the relevant provider/security/performance/verification evidence.
- Unknown facts are not automatically risks accepted by default. The coordinator records them as `UNKNOWN` and either assigns a read-only discovery lane or blocks the dependent decision.
- A moving provider `main` does not itself raise a new implementation risk. Only a material semantic/authority/path conflict changes task validity; exact-head evidence is refreshed at integration.
- This register must be reread at provider design freeze, host selection, candidate freeze and final cutover. New material risks discovered by workers become explicit Issue/evidence updates, not private notes.
